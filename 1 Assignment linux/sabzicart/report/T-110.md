## Stopping Kamran's sync processes
Command: pgrep -af kamran-sync ; pkill -f '^kamran-sync' ; pgrep -af kamran-sync || echo "no kamran-sync processes left" ; ps aux | grep '[k]amran'
Answer: Before: PIDs 60725 and 60726 were running. After pkill -f '^kamran-sync' (the ^ matches only command lines that START with kamran-sync), pgrep prints nothing ("no kamran-sync processes left") and ps shows no kamran processes at all. Note: these two processes had been killed by my laptop's reboot earlier and I restarted them with the setup's helper script, so the piece value they carry is the same.
